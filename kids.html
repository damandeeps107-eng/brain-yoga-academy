How long is the program and who is it for?

A 31-day guided program that teaches children simple daily practices. Continued guidance is provided for one year to help the habits truly stick.

Is this suitable for children in the digital age?

Yes. It is designed for children aged 6–15 to help improve focus and emotional balance in today’s digital environment.

How does this program relate to school education?

No. It complements school education by improving focus, self-control, and learning clarity.

How soon can parents see changes?

Many parents notice improvements in focus, calmness, and self-discipline within a few weeks of consistent practice.

Is transformation instant?

The program’s benefits are there for all to see. You can watch a multitude of videos of children and parents as they   share their experience after following the teachings of the program. Each child is unique and will have their own level of innate development. The advancement or transformation depends on the child’s own efforts and daily practice and parents’ support. The program advises the children to devote 15-25 minutes to home practice every day. With regular practice, children and parents have reported an improvement in their innate abilities. It is the basis of the breakthroughs that the children have experienced.
